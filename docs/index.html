Executive summary

Management takeaways

Evidence to implication
  • Constraint: LITHO is the engineered bottleneck at about 0.85 peak slot utilization, so capacity should be evaluated by whole-line response.
  • Capacity: A LITHO tool gives the largest modeled cycle-time reduction, while its investment-stress cost is higher than the FURNACE option.
  • Quality: The LITHO +1 yield scenario improves flow and raises post-LITHO queue-window violations. Evaluate both outcomes together.
  • Monitoring: EWMA detects the labeled drift on day 84; the output-only monitor stays silent over the 160-day horizon.
  • Dispatch: EDD leads on cycle time and on-time delivery; release control leads on yield risk and total cost in the paired runs.

Data and methodology

Evidence, methods, and boundaries

The source boundary is explicit so measured evidence and controlled experiments stay distinct.

Public production log4TU.ResearchData job-shop dataset

Used for process mining and descriptive flow diagnosis. It is not fab data and does not drive the synthetic experiments.

Synthetic fab modelFixed-seed discrete-event simulation

Uses a stylized re-entrant route and batch furnace. Known bottlenecks and labeled anomaly windows support method checks and paired what-if analysis.

Analytical methods

Process mining, KPI decomposition, utilization and Theory of Constraints evidence, EWMA monitoring, and CRN-paired scenario analysis.

Technologies

Python, Jupyter, pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Plotly, Matplotlib, and a lightweight HTML/CSS/JavaScript dashboard.
